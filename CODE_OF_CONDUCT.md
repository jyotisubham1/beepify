# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in beepify a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
✅ Be respectful and inclusive
✅ Accept constructive feedback gracefully
✅ Focus on what is best for the community
❌ No harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
❌ No discriminatory language or behaviour

## Enforcement
Violations can be reported by opening a private issue or contacting the maintainer.
